Drop your portrait here as:  image1.jpeg

The site loads it from  /img/image1.jpeg  (hero circle + About section).

If your photo currently sits in  src/public/img/image1.jpeg , run this
from the project root:

  mkdir -p public/img
  cp src/public/img/image1.jpeg public/img/image1.jpeg

(Vite only serves files from the root "public/" folder — files inside
"src/" are treated as source code and never reach the browser.)
